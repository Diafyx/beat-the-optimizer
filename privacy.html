Beat the Optimizer

Privacy

There are no accounts and no cookies. The game keeps your progress in your browser, and sends a few anonymous counts so we can tell whether players come back the next day.

What stays in your browser

Your progress is kept in your browser's local storage, under beat-the-optimizer:v1, and never leaves your device:

  • whether you have seen “How to play”;
  • for each daily puzzle you played, the cost of each of your runs;
  • your streak: the last puzzle you played and how many days in a row led up to it;
  • the date you first finished a daily puzzle, and which of the events below have been sent, so each is sent only once.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

What is sent

The game sends counts to GoatCounter:

  • on each visit, the page's path (/, without the level you practise) and the address of the site that linked to it, without the page on that site;
  • first_finish, once, when you finish your first daily puzzle;
  • return_d1, once, if you come back the day after that;
  • return_d2_7, once, if you come back later in that first week.

An event is only its name: it has no identifier, no date, no level and no score.

The site also loads Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits to the site. It reports the page's address, the address that linked to it, your browser's user agent and how long the page took to load.

Like any request on the web, these reach GoatCounter and Cloudflare with your IP address and your browser's user agent. Their privacy policies say what they do with them: GoatCounter and Cloudflare.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, the game sends nothing to GoatCounter or Cloudflare, and does not store the date or the events above.
